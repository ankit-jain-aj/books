I'm a consultant, author and coach with a keen interest in simplifying complex ideas. I've spent over two decades in IT consulting, where I was always drawn to patterns, systems and smart problem-solving.

My first book, **Patterns in the Noise**, began as a personal project: an attempt to decode the logic behind stock market charts and separate signal from noise. I wrote it not as a market guru, but as a fellow learner who asks the same questions beginners do.

My second book, **The Wisdom of Everyday Life**, I wrote with my father, Rakesh Kumar Jain. It gathers the practical principles for a calm, meaningful life that have guided our family. I believe timeless wisdom becomes truly valuable only when it is communicated clearly and put into practice.

I graduated as an engineer in Computer Technology, then completed the Post Graduate Program in Management at IMT Ghaziabad and an MBA (Global) in Strategy and Leadership from Deakin University, Australia.

On an endless journey of learning…
