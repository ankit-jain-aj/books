Rakesh Kumar Jain is a lifelong student of practical wisdom, meditation and self-understanding. He was born and educated in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh, and is an Electrical Engineering graduate of the University of Roorkee (now IIT Roorkee).

He began his career at Durgapur Steel Plant, then served with the Central Electricity Authority and later the Indian Railway Service of Electrical Engineers, retiring in November 2014 as a Senior Administrative Grade Officer.

Since retirement, he has devoted himself to spirituality, reflection and understanding the principles of a peaceful and meaningful life.

He conceived this idea of sharing his learning and wisdom of his spiritual learning with rest of the world and together we gave these learning shape of this book - "The Wisdom of Everyday Life"
