About the book

Life is beautiful, yet often filled with unnecessary suffering. Most of our pain comes not from life itself, but from the way we think, expect, react and hold on.

Drawing from ancient wisdom, philosophy, psychology and everyday experience, this book explores how our thoughts, emotions, expectations, relationships, finances, health and daily choices shape the quality of our lives. It is written in simple language, with relatable stories.

In this book, you will discover

  • How expectations create suffering, and how to let them go
  • Why acceptance is the doorway to peace
  • How thoughts, emotions and beliefs shape your reality
  • The secrets of healthy relationships and meaningful living
  • Practical ways to live wisely in a complex world

"Peace is not something you find outside. It is something you create within."

The 13 Golden Rules

Simple principles from the book to come back to, one day at a time.

1

Maintain realistic expectations

Do your best. Hold outcomes with open hands.

2

Accept what you cannot control

Accept reality. Focus on what you can change.

3

Avoid comparison

Measure your progress against yourself.

4

Think before judging others

You see a moment, not the whole story.

5

Manage anger

Understand the expectation behind anger.

6

Avoid labelling people

People can change. Avoid quick labels.

7

Be present

The present moment is the only moment.

8

Be aware of the mind's interpretations

Notice your interpretation. Check its accuracy.

9

Learn from repeated mistakes

Find the pattern. Choose a better response.

10

Resolve conflicts quickly

Speak sooner. Silence often worsens things.

11

Help others with compassion

Give what you can. Release the outcome.

12

Practise financial discipline

Live within means. Save and prepare for needs.

13

Care for your physical health

Move, eat well, rest, and keep balance.
